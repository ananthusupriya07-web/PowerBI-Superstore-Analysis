# PowerBI-Superstore-Analysis
A Power BI dashboard analyzing Superstore sales performance, including KPIs, trends, customer segmentation, and regional insights.
his is a Power BI dashboard built using the Sample Superstore dataset to analyze key business metrics such as sales performance, customer segmentation, profit trends, and regional contribution.

Key Analysis Areas

Total Sales & Profit Overview

Sales and Profit Trends Over Time

Category and Sub-Category Performance

Customer Segmentation and Region Comparison

Top-Performing and Under-Performing Business Areas
